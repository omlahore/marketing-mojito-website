Absolutely. We have various packages that ensure your site stays secure, up-to-date, and optimized.

Website Development Company in India for Sites That Sell

Marketing Mojito is a website development company in India designing and building marketing websites, e-commerce stores, and landing pages engineered to rank and convert — not just look good in a portfolio. Our web development services span UX and design, development, e-commerce development on Shopify and WooCommerce, and ongoing optimisation.

Your website is the one channel you own outright. Every ad, post, and search result ends there — so its speed, clarity, and conversion path decide the ROI of everything else you spend on marketing.

What our web services include

  • Business & marketing websites — fast, SEO-ready sites with clear conversion paths.
  • E-commerce development — Shopify and WooCommerce stores: catalogue architecture, payments, shipping, and analytics.
  • Landing pages — campaign-specific pages built and iterated with our CRO team.
  • Technical SEO build quality — clean markup, structured data, and Core Web Vitals baked in with our SEO team.
  • Content & visuals — copy, photography, and design produced in-house, so the site launches complete.
  • Care plans — updates, backups, security, and continuous improvement after launch.

Our build process

We begin with structure: who the site must convince and what action they must take. Sitemap and wireframes come before visual design; design comes before code; and everything is tested on real devices before launch. Sites ship with analytics, search indexing, and conversion tracking already configured — launch day is day one of marketing, not week one of fixes.

Frequently asked questions

How much does website development cost in India?

A professional business website typically costs ₹75,000–₹3,00,000 in India; e-commerce builds range ₹1,50,000–₹6,00,000+ depending on catalogue size and integrations. Beware quotes that exclude content, SEO setup, and tracking — those gaps surface later as costs. Our proposals itemise everything.

Shopify or WooCommerce — which should I choose?

Shopify wins on speed-to-launch, reliability, and ease of operation; WooCommerce wins on flexibility and lower recurring platform costs if you can manage hosting. We recommend based on your catalogue, team, and integration needs — not a default platform we resell.

How long does a website take to build?

A marketing site typically takes 4–8 weeks; e-commerce stores 8–14 weeks including catalogue setup and payment/shipping testing. Timelines are fixed in the proposal with content responsibilities agreed upfront — content delay is the #1 cause of late launches.

Will my website rank on Google?

It will launch technically ready to rank: clean structure, fast load, structured data, and indexation handled. Ranking for competitive terms then depends on content and authority — which our SEO team can build as an ongoing programme.
